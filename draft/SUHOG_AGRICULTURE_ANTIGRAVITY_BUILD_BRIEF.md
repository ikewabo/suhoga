# SUHOG AGRICULTURE — ANTIGRAVITY BUILD BRIEF

## PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Extend the existing SuhoG website with a dedicated Agriculture initiative without allowing agriculture to visually or conceptually blur SuhoG's existing care/community-service identity.

The site should communicate:

> **One SuhoG mission. Distinct operating initiatives. Shared community impact.**

Do **not** redesign the entire SuhoG website to look agricultural.  
Do **not** insert farming content randomly between care-service sections.

Instead, build Agriculture as a **special-purpose initiative / impact venture within the SuhoG ecosystem**, with a dedicated landing page, distinct visual world, and clear relationship back to the parent SuhoG brand.

---

# 1. BRAND / ORGANIZATIONAL ARCHITECTURE

Use this conceptual structure:

```text
                         SUHOG
              Community Impact Ecosystem
                         │
          ┌──────────────┼──────────────┐
          │              │              │
          ▼              ▼              ▼
    SUHOG PROJECT   SUHOG NATURALS   SUHOG AGRICULTURE
   Community/Care    Social Enterprise  Impact Initiative
          │              │              │
    Care & Support   VIZZ / FIZZ      Food Security
    Community Work   Products         Farming
    Charity                           Livelihoods
                                      Partnerships
```

### Important

This is a **website/brand architecture**, not a claim that SuhoG Agriculture is already a separately incorporated legal entity.

Present Agriculture as:

**SUHOG AGRICULTURE INITIATIVE**  
*An initiative of SuhoG Project*

This provides separation now and leaves room for the organization to later establish a dedicated subsidiary, SPV, cooperative, social enterprise, or other legal structure if leadership and professional advisers determine that is appropriate.

---

# 2. POSITIONING

Agriculture is NOT another care service.

It is a **community economic-development and food-security initiative** connected to SuhoG's broader mission.

The relationship should be:

**CARE → supports people today**

**AGRICULTURE → builds sustainable livelihoods for tomorrow**

**NATURALS → creates commerce that can help support the wider mission**

These initiatives belong to the same ecosystem but should remain visually and operationally understandable as separate programs.

# 14. VISUAL SEPARATION FROM CARE SERVICES

This is critical.

Care pages can retain their existing SuhoG palette and imagery.

Agriculture receives its own environmental palette derived from imagery:

- deep forest tones
- soil/brown neutrals
- warm sunlight
- muted natural greens
- cream/off-white typography areas

Do not hard-code a completely unrelated brand identity.

SuhoG navigation, logo, typography, glass effects, button behavior, footer, grid, and interaction language should remain consistent.

The visitor should feel:

> "I entered a different SuhoG initiative."

Not:

> "I entered a different company."

---

# 15. MOBILE EXPERIENCE

Mobile is a first-class requirement.

At widths below tablet:

- Hero becomes portrait-friendly.
- Never crop faces awkwardly.
- Headline remains dramatic but does not overflow.
- Numbered cards stack vertically.
- Ecosystem cards become horizontally swipeable or vertically stacked.
- "From Soap to Soil" becomes a controlled vertical transition.
- Impact flow changes from horizontal to vertical.
- Buttons become thumb-friendly.
- Avoid excessive glass blur on low-powered mobile devices.
- Avoid large autoplay media downloads.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

Maintain generous spacing.

No desktop element should simply shrink until unreadable.

---
